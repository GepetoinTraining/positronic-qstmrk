EXACT READINGS AND PREDICTIONS
Profiles along an axis, exact integers: band profile B_i = sum_j 4^band_ij (address set to 1); address profile
A_i = sum_j odd_ij^2 (band removed); full profile F_i = sum_j odd^2 4^band. Zeros contribute 0.
Period excess for T | N: X_T(p) = T * sum_r (sum_k p[r + kT])^2 - (sum p)^2  (integer, >= 0).
A line at T: X_T(real) > X_T of every one of 100 position shuffles of the profile.
