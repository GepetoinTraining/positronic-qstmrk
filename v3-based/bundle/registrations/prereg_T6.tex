\textbf{Object.} Octonions with basis $e_0=1,e_1,\dots,e_7$ and the fixed table $e_ie_{i+1}=e_{i+3}$ (indices mod 7, on $1..7$), i.e.\ lines $(1,2,4),(2,3,5),(3,4,6),(4,5,7),(5,6,1),(6,7,2),(7,1,3)$, cyclic order positive, $e_i^2=-1$, distinct imaginaries anticommuting.
A \emph{labeling} is a Steiner system $S(3,4,8)$ on $\{0,\dots,7\}$ (14 blocks; there are 30). Its candidate unit set $U$ has 240 elements: the 16 units $\pm e_a$, and for each block $\{a,b,c,d\}$ the 16 elements $\tfrac12(\pm e_a\pm e_b\pm e_c\pm e_d)$. The \emph{aligned} labeling has blocks $\{0\}\cup L$ for each line $L$ and their complements.

\textbf{Declarations.} All coordinates carried as integers scaled by 2. No floats. A product is \emph{inside} iff it equals an element of $U$ exactly.

\textbf{Predictions and kills.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[H1] Closure. At least one labeling gives $U$ closed: all $240^2=57{,}600$ products inside. \emph{Kill:} no labeling closes.
\item[H2] The aligned labeling is \emph{not} closed. (Prior from the historical record: Kirmse's 1924 integers, as reported by Coxeter and by Conway--Smith; a recall, not a derivation.) \emph{Kill:} it closes.
\item[H3] Rotation. For a closed $U$, left multiplication by each unit permutes $U$ exactly, and $N(uv)=N(u)N(v)$ holds exactly on all pairs. \emph{Kill:} any unit fails to permute $U$.
\item[H4] Count. Exactly 7 of the 30 labelings close. Low-confidence prior. \emph{Kill:} any other number.
\item[E1] Exploratory, no prediction: for each non-closed $U$, where the fallout products land (inside another labeling's $U$; half-integer coordinates; quarter-integer coordinates).
\end{enumerate}
Outcomes are appended below this line and never edited into the text above.

\hrule\vspace{0.4em}
\textbf{Outcomes} (appended after the run; registration text above unchanged, SHA-256 of the registered source \texttt{b3166908\ldots6fbd}).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[H1] \textbf{Holds.} 7 of the 30 labelings give a closed set: all 57{,}600 products inside.
\item[H2] \textbf{Holds.} The aligned labeling is not closed: 43{,}008 of 57{,}600 products fall outside.
\item[H3] \textbf{Holds.} In a closed set every left multiplication by a unit permutes all 240 exactly; $N(uv)=N(u)N(v)$ exactly on all pairs.
\item[H4] \textbf{Holds.} Exactly 7 of 30 close. The prior was marked low-confidence.
\item[E1] Across all non-closed labelings, fallout products either land exactly inside another labeling's unit set (387{,}072) or leave the half-integer lattice with quarter-integer coordinates (688{,}128). No product lands at half-integer coordinates outside every labeling. For the aligned labeling, all 43{,}008 fallout products are half-integer and every one lands inside another labeling's set.
\end{enumerate}
Sanity checks run first: the table is a composition algebra and left-alternative on 300 random integer pairs.
